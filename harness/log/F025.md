- Saw: on the running app (`./init.sh`, Next 16.4.2, port 3350) in a fresh browser context: /sign-up loaded fresh has 1 `<main>`. After sign-up → /books: 2 `<main>` (1 hidden). After Sign out → /sign-in: 3 `<main>` (2 hidden), 2× `#email`, 2× `#password`, and no textbox named exactly "Email". After Add book → /books/new → back to the list → book page: 5 `<main>` (4 hidden), 2× `#status`, combobox named "Status Status". After clicking Edit: 2× `#title` and 2× `#author`, textboxes named "Title Title" and "Author Author". A reload of the book page gives 1 `<main>`, 1 `#status`, and the names "Status", "Title", "Author".
- Expected: one `<main>` and unique ids in the document, and each field named once.
- Likely cause (not confirmed): `cacheComponents: true` in next.config.ts keeps visited routes mounted and hidden (React Activity). Labels use fixed ids (`htmlFor="status"` in both app/books/new/new-book-form.tsx and components/book-status-form.tsx, the same for title, author, email, password), so a hidden page's element with the same id adds a second label to the name. A fix could make the ids unique (`useId`) and/or stop hidden routes from staying in the DOM. Any change to the cacheComponents setting needs a decision and an architect PASS.
- Belongs to: cross-cutting. It shows up in F002 (auth pages), F010 (status form) and F018 (edit form); filed under M3, the current milestone.
